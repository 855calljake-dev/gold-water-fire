Why reconstruction stays in-house

Once mitigation is complete, most properties still need real construction work: framing, drywall, flooring, paint, and finishes. Gold Water Fire's reconstruction work is led by Construction Manager Brandon Gurr and Reconstruction Team Lead Johnny — so the same company that handled the cleanup handles the rebuild, with no second contractor to coordinate.

Common Questions

Questions people ask us

Who leads Gold Water Fire's reconstruction work?

Construction Manager Brandon Gurr and Reconstruction Team Lead Johnny lead reconstruction and rebuild projects.

Do I need to hire a separate contractor for repairs after mitigation?

No — reconstruction is handled in-house by the same company that does the water or fire mitigation, so there's no second contractor to coordinate.

What areas do you serve for reconstruction?

The greater Phoenix, Arizona metro area, from Peoria and Surprise in the northwest to San Tan Valley and Apache Junction in the southeast, and the cities in between.
