# Imagery References — Gold Water Fire

Internal only, not published (blocked in `netlify.toml` alongside `CLAIMS-TO-VERIFY.md`). A growing
list of real restoration-industry sites with strong photography, kept for **visual style only** —
never their actual photos, copy, or trade dress. Every image we actually use is either a real Gold
Water Fire job photo (none exist yet) or Higgsfield-generated and clearly captioned as illustrative,
per `CLAIMS-TO-VERIFY.md`. This file is prompt inspiration, not an asset source.

## How to use this

When generating a new Higgsfield image for a page, skim this list for the closest matching shot
type, borrow the *composition and mood*, and write an original prompt — never describe someone
else's actual photo closely enough that the output would resemble it.

## Reference sites

### Voda Cleaning & Restoration of Phoenix — myvoda.com/phoenix-az/water-extraction-removal/
*Added 2026-08-07.*

Real company, real photography (their own jobs/team/equipment — not usable, style only). Shot types
present on this one page, confirmed via alt text: "Water Damage Restoration – Drying," "Water
Extraction," "Voda Team," "Voda Equipment." Pattern worth borrowing:

- **Action-in-progress shots**, not posed portraits — a technician mid-task with visible extraction
  or drying equipment (air movers, dehumidifiers), not looking at the camera.
- **Equipment close-ups** as their own standalone image, not just background detail — the hardware
  itself signals competence to a homeowner who's never seen a restoration job before.
- **Team photos** exist but are secondary to the action shots, not the hero.
- Water-specific: hoses, extraction wands, visible standing water or wet flooring in frame.

Preferred Choice Restoration (checked earlier in this build) confirmed the same pattern at the
page-layout level: sticky emergency CTA, repeated phone number, trust badges, service-area
specificity. Not re-logged here since it's already in `CLAIMS-TO-VERIFY.md`'s build history — this
file is for *imagery* specifically, that one was for layout.

## Prompt patterns proven to work (from this project's own generations)

- **Water damage:** technician + air movers + dehumidifier + visible wet flooring/standing water,
  natural window light, documentary photojournalism style, no visible face, no branding/logos on
  clothing or equipment.
- **Fire damage:** two-person crew removing smoke-damaged drywall, soot-covered structural element
  (brick fireplace worked well) in frame, protective masks/gloves, work lights for contrast.
- **Reconstruction:** single figure framing/finishing work, clean modern interior mid-renovation,
  natural light, visible tools/materials.
- **Emergency/hero mood:** night scene, service vehicle with work lights on, caution tape,
  wet/reflective pavement, distant out-of-focus emergency personnel (never legible faces or
  department branding — checked every time before use).

## Guardrails, every time (not optional, not a suggestion)

1. No real recognizable people, no legible badges/department logos/company branding belonging to
   anyone else.
2. Never caption a generated image as an actual completed Gold Water Fire job — "Illustrative
   imagery" note stays on every page that uses one, per `CLAIMS-TO-VERIFY.md`.
3. Don't copy a specific claim visible in a reference site's imagery (e.g. a badge reading "IICRC
   Certified" in someone else's photo is not license to put that badge in ours — that claim is
   separately gated and currently unconfirmed for Gold Water Fire).
