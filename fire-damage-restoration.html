The Process

What fire damage restoration covers

Assessment and board-up

We assess the extent of fire, smoke, and water damage from firefighting efforts, and secure the property.

Smoke and soot removal

Affected surfaces, contents, and structural materials are cleaned or removed.

Odor control

Treatment to address smoke odor throughout the affected structure.

What's Next

From cleanup to rebuild

Fire damage often means structural repair, not just cleanup. Our reconstruction team handles framing, drywall, and finish work to bring the property back — one company, start to finish.

Common Questions

Questions people ask us

Is smoke odor removed along with visible soot?

Yes — odor treatment is part of the fire damage process, addressing smoke odor throughout the affected structure, not just the visibly burned areas.

Can you also rebuild what was damaged in the fire?

Yes. Gold Water Fire's reconstruction team, led by Construction Manager Brandon Gurr, handles framing, drywall, and finish work after cleanup, so the whole job stays with one company.

What areas do you serve for fire damage restoration?

The greater Phoenix, Arizona metro area, from Peoria and Surprise in the northwest to San Tan Valley and Apache Junction in the southeast, and the cities in between.
