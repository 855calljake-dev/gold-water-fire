The Process

What water damage restoration covers

Water extraction

Standing water removed from floors, carpet, and structural cavities as soon as our crew arrives.

Structural drying

Equipment placed and monitored to dry the structure and reduce the risk of secondary damage.

Cleanup and prep

Affected materials cleaned or removed, and the space prepared for repair or reconstruction if needed.

Common Causes

What we respond to

Burst or leaking pipes, water heater failures, washing machine and dishwasher supply-line failures, roof and plumbing leaks, and flooding. If a job also needs rebuild work — flooring, drywall, cabinetry — our own reconstruction team picks it up without bringing in a second contractor.

Common Questions

Questions people ask us

How fast does water damage need to be addressed?

Standing water and wet building materials support mold growth within 24–48 hours, so extraction and drying should start as soon as possible after the source of water is stopped.

Do you handle both the water damage and any repairs afterward?

Yes. Gold Water Fire's own reconstruction team handles framing, drywall, and flooring repair after mitigation, so you're not coordinating a second contractor.

What areas do you serve for water damage restoration?

The greater Phoenix, Arizona metro area, from Peoria and Surprise in the northwest to San Tan Valley and Apache Junction in the southeast, and the cities in between.
